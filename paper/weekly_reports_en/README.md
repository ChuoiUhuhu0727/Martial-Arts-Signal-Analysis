# Weekly Reports — Week 5 to Week 10

Reports of **what was done and what came out of it** (not plans), compiled from the git
commit history, `CHANGELOG.md` and the `README.md` progress log.

**How the weeks are divided:** the actual git log shows the work concentrated into about
six real calendar weeks (27 June – 20 August) rather than spread evenly across nine weeks
as originally scheduled. The content is therefore **grouped by topic and logical order**,
staying as close as possible to the original theme of each week in
[`../../archived/project_description.md`](../../archived/project_description.md), rather
than matched to exact calendar dates.

## Relationship with the thesis

The two documents **complement each other rather than replace each other**:

- **The thesis** (`../THESIS_EN.md`) is organised by **argument**: problem → root cause →
  solution. It tells you *what the final conclusion is*.
- **The weekly reports** are organised by **time**: what got done each week. They tell you
  *how the conclusion changed along the way* — something the thesis cannot demonstrate
  about itself, because it was written after the answer was known.

The clearest example sits inside [Week 10](week_10.md) itself: part 1 presents the
three-filter comparison with confidence, and part 3 overturns that very result after the
reference measurement is found to be broken. That order is **kept as it happened, not tidied
up** — the process of finding and correcting one's own mistake is part of the research
result.
